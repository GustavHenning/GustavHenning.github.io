Hi, I'm Gustav. I'm a computer scientist from Sweden.

I spend a lot of my time thinking about intelligence and consciousness: what they are, how they arise, and whether we can build or understand them. My biggest wish in life is to contribute something to those questions.

## About this site

This is a place where I express my personal thoughts. It is organised by [topic]({{ '/topics/' | relative_url }}) rather than by date, and pages get revised as my thinking changes instead of being replaced by new ones. Nothing here is a final position, and the seeds in particular are allowed to be wrong.

## How to read it

- Pick a [topic]({{ '/topics/' | relative_url }}) and follow the links.
- Each note has an infobox with its status and when it was last revised.
- The bottom of every note lists the other notes that link to it.
- The [log]({{ '/log/' | relative_url }}) shows what has changed recently.
