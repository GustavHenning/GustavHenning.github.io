## One idea per note

A note argues for or explores one thing. If a section grows large enough to stand alone, it becomes its own note and the original links to it.

## Status over apology

Rather than hedging in the text, each note declares a status in its infobox: *seed*, *growing*, or *evergreen*. A seed is allowed to be wrong.

## Link generously

Ideas rarely belong to one topic. Notes link to each other freely, and every note lists the pages that link back to it. See [what I mean by philosophy]({{ '/philosophy/what-this-topic-is-about/' | relative_url }}) for an example of a seed.

## Revise, don't republish

When I change my mind, I edit the note and bump the *revised* date. The [log]({{ '/log/' | relative_url }}) shows what has moved recently.
