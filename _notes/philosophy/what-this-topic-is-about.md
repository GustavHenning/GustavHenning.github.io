When I file something under *philosophy* here, I mean questions that do not resolve by measuring something. How should I live? What do I owe other people? What does it mean for a belief to be justified?

## Why keep notes on it

Writing a position down forces me to notice where it is vague. Revising it later shows me where I have changed my mind, which is usually more interesting than the position itself.

## What to expect

Most notes here will start as seeds: a claim, a question, and a few lines of reasoning. See [how I take notes here]({{ '/philosophy/how-i-take-notes/' | relative_url }}) for the conventions.
